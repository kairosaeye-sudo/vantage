About

Serving Austin and nearby

At Chapman Electric, we are proud to be your local electrical contractor in Austin and Spicewood, TX, providing safe, reliable, and high-quality electrical services for homes and businesses alike. Founded by Alex and Kayla Chapman, who were raised in entrepreneurial families, our company is built on integrity, faith, and a passion for serving the community.

Alex discovered his love for electrical work at just 12 years old and turned it into a lifelong career. He started as an electrician straight out of high school and has loved every moment since. With Alex’s natural leadership, strong people skills, and focus on building relationships, Chapman Electric is more than a business—it’s a team that genuinely cares about the people we serve.

Our goal is to combine skill, professionalism, and a personal touch so that every customer feels valued. From your first phone call to project completion, we make sure your experience is smooth, transparent, and stress-free.
