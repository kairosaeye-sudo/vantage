About

Serving Austin since 1987

Beckett Electrical Services is a family-owned electrical contractor that has served the Austin area for over three decades. Founded in 1987, we built our reputation on honest pricing, quality workmanship, and showing up when we say we will.

Every electrician on our team is licensed, insured, and background-checked. We handle everything from simple outlet repairs to full commercial buildouts, and we treat every home like it is our own.

We are fully licensed in the state of Texas (License #12345) and carry comprehensive liability insurance on every job.
